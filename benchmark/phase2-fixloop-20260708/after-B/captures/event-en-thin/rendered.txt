ExtraSauce 002 lands at Bios Romantso on Saturday 30 May 2026 at 23:00 — the second edition of Manliketank's bass-music night, four months after January's debut at Romantso.

Genre brief: full-spectrum bass — UKG, funky, breaks, dubstep, grime, jungle. The opening edition pulled poorjdarr, x_polyxene, and ceptal_ alongside Manliketank, setting the slot's character.

Bios sits at Pireos 84, a Bauhaus building in Gazi a 10-minute walk from Kerameikos metro. The basement carries a Funktion-One rig — the room you want when bass is the point. Cocktails run EUR 9, door relaxed.
