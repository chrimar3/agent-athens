I Leksi is a world-premiere opera at the Greek National Opera's Alternative Stage in Kallithea, Athens, on 18 April 2026. Composer and conductor Alkis Baltas scores the final hours of Renaissance philosopher Giordano Bruno, burned at the stake in 1600 for refusing to renounce his theories on infinite universes.

Baritone Tassis Christogiannopoulos carries I Leksi across four scenes with a chamber trio of violin, cello, and harpsichord. Eleni Zafirio's libretto is sung in Greek. The production runs eighty minutes with no interval.

The SNFCC shuttle from Syntagma drops you at the door. Tickets EUR 10-20.
