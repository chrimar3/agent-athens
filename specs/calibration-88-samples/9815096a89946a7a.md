Mama Athens is a recurring electronic DJ night at B-Side in Omonoia, Athens, on 17 April 2026. The series has held residence at Mavrokordatou 6 since the bar opened in 2018, sharing the building with Cannibal Radio and drawing from the local techno and house scene that orbits both.

This edition pairs DEF and Homo Erectio behind the decks from 23:00. B-Side operates as a single dark room built for sound rather than scenery, with a cocktail menu organized by audio frequency — Bass, Mid, Treble — and entry that remains open.

Two local selectors, one late-night room, and a party series that outlasts most Athens club nights by years rather than months.
