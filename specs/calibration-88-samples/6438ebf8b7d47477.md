Awaken the Dreamer vol IV is a live tribute to Warrel Dane, the Sanctuary and Nevermore vocalist who died in December 2017, now in its fourth consecutive year at AN Club in Exarchia.

Bigus Dickus, the tribute's anchor act, reunites with guest musicians from the Greek metal scene to perform Dane's catalogue spanning both bands. Opening act Nymphis play songs from their debut, with a DJ set from Nikos Tragakis filling the gaps. The fourth edition lands on 18 April 2026, keeping a tradition that has outlasted most annual metal gatherings in Athens.

Doors open ahead of a late start typical of AN Club. Admission is EUR 12.
