The Jesse Davis Quartet is a jazz concert at Half Note Jazz Club in Mets, Athens, on April 17, 2026. The room holds two hundred people and the tables press close enough to the stage that you hear Davis breathe between phrases.

Davis is a New Orleans-born alto saxophonist who studied under Ellis Marsalis at the New Orleans Center for Creative Arts and arrived in New York in the late 1980s as part of the young lion generation, playing alongside Cedar Walton, Benny Golson, and Wynton Marsalis across a career now approaching four decades. His latest album Reflections, released April 3 on Cellar Music Group's SmallsLIVE Living Masters Series, grounds the quartet in groove-oriented hard bop.

Half Note has hosted roughly 250 concerts per season since 1979 and enforces a strict reservation policy — book ahead, confirm by phone the same day, and arrive twenty minutes early or lose your table fifteen minutes after showtime. The venue is a ten-minute walk from Akropoli metro, but the 22:30 Friday start outlasts the last train.

Two weeks into a new album cycle, heard in a room built for exactly this.
