Ta Genethlia tis Ririkas is a children's musical theater piece by Maria Kilaidoni, now in its third consecutive season at Theatro Metaxourgeio. A bear named Ririka celebrates her birthday alone after moving to a new mountain and heads to the city for pistachio ice cream — only to find the shop closed. Along the way she meets porcupines, a crocodile pastry chef, lion musicians, and a kangaroo dancer.

Kilaidoni wrote the story, composed the music, and performs alongside Alexandros Lykouras and Michalis Petridis. You get 55 minutes of live singing and physical comedy, sized for ages two to ten.

Theatro Metaxourgeio at Akadimou 14 is a 114-seat space founded in 1999 by Loukianos Kilaidonis — Maria's father. If you arrive early, every seat has a clear sightline. Sundays at 12:00 through 24 May. Tickets EUR 12.

A daughter performing her own work in the theater her father built — that continuity is the real story.
