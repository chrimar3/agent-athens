From Stalingrad to Berlin is a concert marking the 120th anniversary of Dmitri Shostakovich's birth, held at the Parnassos Literary Society on 20 April 2026.

The programme draws from Shostakovich's wartime and post-war output -- symphonic works, marches, and film soundtracks composed between the siege of Stalingrad and the fall of Berlin. Parnassos, a neoclassical hall from 1899 on Agiou Georgiou Karytsi Square, seats roughly 500 across its main floor and gallery, with acoustics shaped by 19th-century ceiling panels that predate electrification.

Doors open at 19:30. Tickets are EUR 15, available through ticketservices.gr.
