Oi Theoi tou Olympou kai i Gennisi tou Kosmou is a children's theater production written and directed by Carmen Rouggeri at Christmas Theater in Galatsi, retelling how the world emerged from chaos and how the Olympian gods came to power.

Rouggeri, who founded the Children's Stage at both the National Theater and the National Lyric Stage in the 1990s, structures the show as a chain of origin stories: Earth and Sea splitting from void, Zeus overthrowing Kronos, Prometheus stealing fire, Athena winning Athens from Poseidon. Each episode introduces a different god with its own dramatic logic rather than a single linear plot.

The production opened in autumn 2025 and continues into spring 2026, past its originally announced closing date. Christmas Theater, a converted Olympic hall seating up to 3,600, gives the staging room that smaller children's venues cannot match. If your children fidget in intimate spaces, the scale here works in your favor. If you prefer cozy, this is not that.

Tickets range from EUR 10 to EUR 30 by zone.
