FM is a British AOR band returning to Athens to play their 1986 debut Indiscreet in full, marking the album's 40th anniversary. The record shaped melodic rock with tracks like Frozen Heart and That Girl — the latter covered by Iron Maiden. Their latest album Brotherhood dropped in September 2025, proving the band still records as well as it tours.

Kyttaro on Ipirou Street has hosted rock acts since the early 1970s, and its 800-capacity room suits a night built around a single album played front to back. Doors at 19:30, tickets EUR 30 via more.com.
