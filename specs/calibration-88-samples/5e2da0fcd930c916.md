The Organ-ization Sessions is a concert at Poetry Bar in Athens on April 18, 2026, pairing a Hammond organ trio with guest saxophonist Yiannis Kassetas. Panagiotis Smarnakas on Hammond, Giorgos Koutsikoulis on guitar, and Panagiotis Thema on drums work a format born in Harlem clubs where organ and guitar trade groove for harmony in real time.

Kassetas, whom American saxophonist Craig Handy called "the jazz Ennio Morricone of Greece," brings a composer's ear to what is fundamentally a blowing session. His album East N Blues won the Jazz On Line Audience Award in 2014.

Poetry Bar on Soultani 4 seats fewer than fifty — close enough to hear the Leslie cabinet rotate. Doors at 21:00, no reservations.
