Filippos + Decoup + Baby Jones is a three-DJ set at Dybbuk in Kolonaki, Athens, on Wednesday 23 April 2026. The bass reaches your chest before you finish the stairs down.

Filippos has released on Connected and Madorasindahouse, labels where Afro house meets deep house — layered selections rather than peak-time bangers. Decoup works a similar palette with a producer's ear for texture. Baby Jones rounds out a bill that reads like a mid-week warm-up but runs until dawn. Dybbuk has held its ground since 2009 as Kolonaki's house and techno outpost, and this Wednesday lineup leans into the venue's deeper identity.

If you want big-room techno, Dybbuk is a living room, not a warehouse. If you want house music in a room where the DJ sees every face, this is your Wednesday.

Dybbuk sits at Patriarchou Ioakim 37, five minutes from Evangelismos metro. Door at 23:00, ten to fifteen euros. Arriving after 01:00 means fitting into gaps rather than finding space.
