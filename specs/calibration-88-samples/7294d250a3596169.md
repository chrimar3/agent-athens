Around the World in Five Fairy Tales is a children's theater production at Theater 104 in Gazi, Athens, on April 19, 2026. Forty puppets tumble from a box — Anansi the spider, a boy with a magic brush, a frog who drank every river in Australia.

Paramythochora — sisters Maria and Spyridoula Papageorgiou — built each puppet by hand, matching technique and material to each continent's storytelling tradition. Shadow theater, marionettes, black-light animation, and original music by Dimitris Oikonomakis carry five folk tales without dialogue that requires Greek. The production targets ages two to five, though older siblings stay put.

If your child sits still for picture books, they will be absorbed. If they need room to move, no seat in the house is far from the action.
