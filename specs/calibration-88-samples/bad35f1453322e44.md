ClubKid x Fragk (Useless Co.) is a DJ set at Crust in Psyrri, Athens, on Saturday April 18. You descend the narrow stairs beneath a pizza counter on Protogenous 13 and the bass meets you halfway down.

ClubKid has held a residency in this basement since 2018, building eight years of Saturday-night trust with a room that holds fewer than a hundred. Fragk brings the other half of Useless Co. — a duo threading garage and breakbeat through house and techno grooves since 2016.

Crust sits two minutes from Monastiraki metro. Doors at midnight, and the slice counter upstairs stays open for when you surface.

Two Psyrri residents splitting a basement and a decade of shared floor time.
