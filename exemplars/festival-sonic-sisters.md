Sonic Sisters Festival returns to Daddy's All Day Bar in Psyri for its second edition — seven hours of music across a single Sunday, open admission, no cover. You walk into the corner bar on Navarchou Apostoli and the decks are already live, the speakers at close range, the room already warm.

The festival was born in March 2026 as a platform for women shaping Athens's DJ and music culture, landing each year on the weekend before International Women's Day. Its creator, Ladydust — the alias of Marina Tsigonaki, a selector with over seven hundred sets across forty cities and a weekly broadcast on Athens Free Radio's Planet Ladydust — conceived a format where different aesthetics, generations, and corners of the scene fold into a single unbroken sound arc. Rock bleeds into electronica, dark disco pivots to synth, indie tangles with underground club.

You will share the room with people who argue about B-sides over negronis at four in the afternoon and know which Psyri basement used to host a record swap. Regulars from the city's darkwave circles sit elbow-to-elbow with electronic music journalists and the sort of quiet obsessives who maintain decade-old genre blogs. They nod along, they talk between sets, they stay.

The debut edition threaded five selectors across that seven-hour arc: Fo, co-founder of the HER project championing women in Athenian electronic music; LOO, founding member of Berlin Brides and Someone Who Isn't Me; Nat K, drawing on twenty-five years in Athens's alternative venues; Red On Rush, a duo rooted in new wave and minimal synth; and Ladydust closing her own curation. Expect the second edition to follow the same logic — a curated sequence, not a random bill. Each selector occupies a chapter.

If you want a headline act and a four-hundred-person queue, this is not the room. But if you want to stand close enough to watch a selector choose her next record, surrounded by people who came for the music and not the guest list, Psyri has cleared seven hours for you.

Daddy's All Day Bar is at Navarchou Apostoli 10, a three-to-five-minute walk from Monastiraki metro. Doors at 20:30, open admission. The bar runs drinks and food through the night. Dress for a long stay.

A festival built around the idea that women selectors deserve not a token slot but the entire evening.
