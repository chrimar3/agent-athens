## Structure

All exemplars follow the 8-section structure, adapted by word count tier:

1. **Citation Anchor** — Declarative first sentence for AI citability
2. **Sensory Opening** — "You" in a specific moment, physical details
3. **Credentials** — Artist/director background, specific works, collaborators
4. **Tribe** — Who attends, why they come, what binds them
5. **Experience** — What you'll actually see/hear/feel during the event
6. **Filter** — "If you [don't want X]... But if you [want Y]..." self-selection
7. **Logistics** — Metro, address, times, ticket prices, practical tips
8. **Closer** — One-line scarcity/uniqueness statement

Shorter tiers (120-180w, 200-300w) compress or omit Tribe and Experience sections. See `theater-tartuffe.md` for the minimal three-block approach.

## Pattern Watch

### Opening Diversity
The five exemplars demonstrate different entry points:
- **Swinton**: Collaborative framing — two names, one project, institutional context
- **Charoulis**: Sound cutting through conversation, crowd falling quiet from recognition
- **LPO/Jarvi**: Architectural scale — seat count, pipe organ, silence before downbeat
- **Tartuffe**: Venue history meeting character — monument cinema, 1664 con artist
- **Sonic Sisters**: Return format — second edition, open admission, already live

Subagents must vary openings across a batch. If two events are in similar venues, find different sensory anchors.

### Tribe Section
**Show behavior, not segments.** Don't list audience types ("theater regulars, music fans, gallery visitors"). Instead, show what people do in the space. Best examples:
- **Charoulis**: "Palms strike tabletops in time. Someone will stand."
- **Sonic Sisters**: "people who argue about B-sides over negronis at four in the afternoon"

### Closer
**Demonstrate scarcity with structural facts.** Don't state "this is rare" — show WHY:
- **Charoulis**: "Thirteen years since he last stood on this particular stage. Four nights to make up for it."
- **LPO/Jarvi**: "A touring orchestra of this caliber, a conductor who owns Sibelius, and a pianist returning to the concerto that made his name"
- **Tartuffe**: "Moliere has been dead for 353 years and still has not run out of hypocrites to write about."

One clause. No restatement of earlier points.

### Sensory Extrapolation Boundary
**Extrapolate from verified facts; never invent from nothing.** If you can't find a source for a venue detail, use the event's sound, performer action, or audience energy instead.
